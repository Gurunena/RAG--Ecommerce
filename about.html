Why we built Consult

Shopping advice, not a search box.

Sri Lanka's electronics market has thousands of near-identical listings and almost no honest comparison. Consult reads the specs so you don't have to, and says so plainly when something doesn't fit.

Our mission

Every recommendation should be something we'd actually give a friend — grounded in real specs, real warranty terms, and real seller reputation, never in who pays the highest placement fee. When nothing in the catalogue genuinely fits, we say that instead of pushing the closest match.

How the AI consultant works

You describe what you need in your own words — budget, purpose, how long it has to last. The consultant asks a clarifying question if something important is missing, then checks the request against verified seller data: RAM, battery life, warranty length, stock, and rating. It returns two or three options, each with a plain-language reason for the fit and the trade-off you're accepting.

Underneath, every listing still comes from real sellers across the island — Consult never manufactures or drop-ships anything itself. It's the layer that makes comparing them honest.

One cart, one payment, real protection

Buying from three different sellers used to mean three checkouts and three separate risks. Consult lets you pay once for a cart that spans every shop, splits it automatically into the right sub-orders, and holds payment in escrow until each parcel is confirmed delivered — so a seller can't take your money and vanish.
