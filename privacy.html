Privacy Policy

This is placeholder policy text for the purposes of this coursework build — structurally complete, not legally reviewed.

1. Data we collect

When you create an account, browse the catalogue, or place an order, Consult may collect:

  • Account details — name, email address, phone number, and password (stored securely, never in plain text on a real deployment).
  • Order information — delivery address, items purchased, and payment method selected (we do not store full card numbers).
  • Usage data — pages visited, products viewed, and messages sent to the shopping consultant, used to improve recommendations.
  • Device information — browser type and approximate location, used only for fraud prevention and basic analytics.

2. How we use it

Your data is used to fulfil orders, personalise product recommendations, communicate order updates, and prevent fraud. Sellers only ever see the shipping details needed to fulfil an order — never your email address or payment details. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

3. Data retention

Account and order data is retained for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to satisfy accounting and dispute-resolution obligations. You may request deletion at any time via the contact page.

4. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Requests can be made through your account settings or by contacting us directly.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@consult.lk or via the contact page.
